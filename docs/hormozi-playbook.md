## $100M Offers — what we take

### Grand Slam Offers (Section I)

A normal offer competes on price and looks like everyone else's. A Grand Slam Offer is the combination of price, value, guarantee, and name that makes saying no feel foolish. It does not take more effort than a weak offer. It takes a better connection to what the audience already wants.

### Pricing and the starving crowd (Section II)

Commodities race to the bottom. Charge in proportion to value, in a market that is already looking. Mosaic's starving crowd is Canadians who find advisors too expensive ($3,000–$10,000/year), robo-advisors too passive, and generic AI too generic — and who still cannot see whether this month moved the right direction.

We do **not** raise Progress off $17 to look premium. The archived plan is right: $17 sits beside YNAB/Monarch in CAD, and we lack bank sync. The next rung ($26) is earned after budgets prove sticky or sync ships. All prices stay on the digit-sum-8 ladder in AGENTS.md.

### The value equation (Section III)

```
Value ≈ (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood of achievement)
        / (Time delay × Effort and sacrifice)
```

Raise the top. Shrink the bottom.

| Variable | Mosaic move |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Know every week whether your money is moving the right direction — Canadian rules, one score |
| Likelihood | Live Score from *their* logs; 7-day trial so they see a report before a card; Guarantee if they do the work and the Score does not move |
| Time delay | First log in the first session; first report the same session they finish the fact-find (real-time, never queued) |
| Effort | Keypad / balance check / statement parse instead of a spreadsheet; Charlie explains, they do not have to become a planner |

### Problems → solutions → trim and stack (Section III)

List every problem on the way to the dream outcome, invent a solution for each, then cut anything that does not move the equation and stack the rest as the offer plus bonuses.

Mosaic problem stack (already in marketing): too expensive, too passive, too generic, picture-in-the-head instead of a picture on a screen. Solutions we keep: free tracking, Canadian calculators, Score, Charlie, Progress Report, statement parse. Solutions we do not stack onto launch: Mastery, Club, Academy, tax pack (built, hidden until Phase 4).

### Enhancers (Section IV)

| Enhancer | Mosaic |
|---|---|
| Scarcity | First 200 founding Progress seats at $8 / $80, locked for life. Live counter. |
| Urgency | Founding deadline = launch day + 14 days, whichever cap hits first. Trial clock is 7 days. |
| Bonuses | (1) Canadian Money Mechanics Toolkit — FHSA, RRSP vs TFSA, CPP timing calculators. (2) Life-Event Playbooks. (3) Founding badge + roadmap input. Tax pack and Club are Mastery bonuses, not launch bonuses. |
| Guarantee | Consistency Guarantee — action-based Win Your Money Back: log weekly + snapshot monthly for 90 days; if the Score has not improved, full refund. Never returns, never "dollars saved," never "follow action steps." Separate 30-day no-questions refund on the first payment. |
| Naming | **The 90-Day Canadian Money Reset.** Progress / Mastery are vehicles, not the name of the offer. |

### Execution (Section V)

One Grand Slam is enough to change the business. We already wrote most of this offer in the archived plan. The gap is presentation and the loop behind it, not a new product.

## $100M Leads — what we take

### Leads are not enough (Section II)

A lead has the problem and the money. They still need an offer and a reason to start. Our lead magnet is something they can use with zero Mosaic literacy: the RRSP/TFSA/FHSA guide and the three calculators. Pulse is the second magnet — a free habit, not a lite paid product.

### Core Four (Section III)

Do these in order. "More, better, new" applies inside a channel before opening the next.

1. **Warm outreach.** People who already know us: waitlist, personal network, anyone who downloaded the guide. Rule of 100: 100 honest reach-outs or conversations a day until founding seats move. This is Stage 1 advertising. No ads required.
2. **Post free content.** The Money Mosaic: faceless, real voiceover, same visual system, worked Canadian numbers. Trust substitutes for a face. Never post generator output verbatim. One CTA per piece.
3. **Cold outreach.** Only after warm + content have a weekly rhythm. Personalized, problem-specific, to advisors, credit unions, and HR benefits leads who already talk money with Canadians. Not spray-and-pray DMs.
4. **Paid ads.** Last. Allowed when we know week-2 log rate and when $170 annual covers CAC inside 30 days. Until then, paid hides a broken loop.

### Lead getters (Section IV)

Other people get leads for us.

| Getter | Mosaic |
|---|---|
| Customer referrals | "Give a month, get a month." Trigger at a win (Score up, report delivered). Reward on the *referred user's paid conversion*, not signup. Dark until ~100 paying. |
| Employees | Not a Stage 0/1 lever. Founder does the Rule of 100. |
| Agencies | Do not hire a media buyer before the money model pays back in 30 days. |
| Affiliates / partners | Advisors ("works alongside your advisor"), credit unions, HR. They get a code and a picture they can share. We do not send them client dollar amounts. Media-arm affiliates (banks, brokerages) fund content; they do not receive Mosaic user data. |

### Open to goal (Section V)

Pick a daily lead number, then fill it with the Core Four in order. Until Phase 2, the goal is waitlist → founding seats, not "followers."

## $100M Money Models — what we take

### Definition (Section I)

A money model is a **sequence of offers**: what we offer, when, and how they pay, so one customer produces enough gross profit in **30 days** to get and serve the next. Credit cards are an interest-free 30-day float if we clear the balance. That is why we care about annual prepay ($170) more than $17/month drip.

A $100M money model makes **one** customer pay for **many** more inside those 30 days. We are not there. We are building Stage I.

### Four offer types (Sections II–V)

| Type | Job | Mosaic mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Attraction | Turn strangers into customers | 7-day reverse trial (free Progress). Consistency Guarantee (Win Your Money Back, actions not outcomes). Founding $8 (discount). Pulse as decoy: do it yourself free, or Charlie + report for $17 / $170. |
| Upsell | More cash when the next problem appears | Annual at report delivery (pay less now). Statement parsing when keypad fatigue appears. Mastery at the 90-day Guarantee win (Club, check-ins). |
| Downsell | Turn a no into a smaller yes | Cancel → Pulse (keep the loop). Payment-plan style: monthly if they refuse annual. Feature: Progress without Mastery. |
| Continuity | Keep buying | Annual Progress. Mastery monthly/annual. Club as the reason Mastery is a different thing, not more chats. |

### Attraction plays we are using

- **Win Your Money Back** — Guarantee. Actions: weekly log + monthly snapshot. Result we measure: Score. Refund if they did the actions and the Score did not improve.
- **Decoy** — Pulse vs Progress. Same tracking; paid adds thinking.
- **Pay less now or pay more later** — $170/year vs $17 × 12. Display annual first.
- **Buy X get Y free** — founding: lock $8 for life (first 200). Not "first 200 lock $17" (toothless if $17 stays list).

### Three stages (Section VI)

1. **Get cash** — attraction that covers cost of getting the customer.
2. **Get more cash** — upsells and downsells so 30-day profit is well above CAC + delivery.
3. **Get the most cash** — continuity and referrals.

Perfect one offer until it is automatic, then add the next. Do not implement the full model in one PR. Raise new offer prices only after yeses are reliable.

### Affiliates fill gaps

If we do not want to deliver a thing (bank sync, insurance review, moving money), we can send people to someone who does and take a disclosed referral. Never a security recommendation. Never a third party that ingests Mosaic user data.

## Our Grand Slam Offer

**Name:** The 90-Day Canadian Money Reset.

**Promise (safe):** Log spending weekly and take a monthly net-worth snapshot for 90 days. See your Financial Health Score move. If you do the work and the Score does not improve, you get your money back. Educational tracking, not advice.

**Dream outcome:** A Canadian who can open Mosaic any week and know whether they are paying down debt, building savings, or drifting — without paying an advisor $3,000–$10,000/year and without pasting their life into a generic chatbot.

**The stack**

| Layer | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Pulse (decoy / downsell / free forever) | Tracking, budgets, net worth, manual holdings, goals, live Score, streaks, calculators | $0 |
| 7-day reverse trial | Full Progress, no card | $0 then ask |
| Progress (volume tier) | Fact-find, 8-section Progress Report + PDF, Charlie (~100 messages/month, silent cap), statement/receipt parsing, monthly regen | $17 / $170; founding $8 / $80 |
| Mastery (anchor, hidden) | Club, quarterly check-ins, priority, tax pack; Academy on annual | $44 / $350 |
| Academy standalone (later) | Courses in the same Club | $26 / $260 |

**Guarantee wording (canonical):** "Log your spending weekly and complete your monthly net-worth snapshot for 90 days. If your Financial Health Score has not improved, full refund." Plus 30-day no-questions refund on the first payment.

**Why this is a Grand Slam when presented correctly**

- Likelihood goes up because they see the Score on their own numbers before they pay (trial) and they keep a refund path if they show up for 90 days.
- Time and effort go down because the first action is a log, not a 20-minute consultation (dashboard-first as of `39f4037`).
- Scarcity and urgency are real (200 seats, 14-day deadline).
- We never promise investment returns or a dollar amount saved.

**Current presentation gap:** the Guarantee is supporting copy; the hero still leads with category language; How It Works still leads with Charlie. Phase 1A in the roadmap is the fix.

## Our lead strategy

Owner of the daily number: the founder, until Phase 3 has a content rhythm.

| Order | Channel | Asset | CTA | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm | Waitlist, guide downloaders, personal list | Signup + 7-day trial (or guide if they will not) | Founder · Rule of 100 |
| 2 | Content | The Money Mosaic shorts + newsletter | Guide or calculator → signup | Founder + later editor |
| 3 | Partners | Advisors, credit unions, HR | Shared code, "show up prepared" | Founder, after weekly content exists |
| 4 | Paid | Only with 30-day payback on $170 | Same offer as warm | Not before Phase 5 |

**Lead magnets already built:** RRSP/TFSA/FHSA PDF on waitlist, `/calculators/fhsa`, `/calculators/rrsp-vs-tfsa`, `/calculators/cpp-timing`, Pulse itself.

**Missing:** PostHog so we can see signup → first log → week-2 log. Without week-2 log rate we are guessing. That is Phase 1C.

**Do not:** run paid to hide a loop where people never log; sell the Club standalone; add a pixel that receives financial free-text.

## Our money model

### What exists today

| Piece | Status |
|---|---|
| Pulse as decoy and as cancel destination | Built (entitlements) |
| 7-day reverse trial | Built |
| Consistency Guarantee (action-based) | Endpoint + settings card; not the public headline |
| Founding $8 / $80, cap 200 | Built ([src/lib/founding.ts](../src/lib/founding.ts)) |
| Annual $170 | Built; pricing UI still defaults monthly |
| Progress / Mastery / Academy Stripe paths | Built; Mastery hidden |
| Referral | Not built |
| Cancel downsell (keep them on a cheaper continuity) | **Decision needed** (billing) |
| Upsell at report delivery / keypad fatigue | Not built |
| Analytics for 30-day payback | Not built |

### Target sequence (do not ship as one PR)

1. Stranger hits a calculator or the guide → signup (Attraction).
2. Lands on the Score loop, logs this week (activation).
3. Trial unlocks Charlie + report. Report in-session (Attraction premium).
4. Checkout defaults annual $170, founding $80 if seats remain (Pay less now).
5. If they refuse annual → monthly $17 (Downsell).
6. If they refuse paid → Pulse forever (Downsell / decoy). They keep logging.
7. After first report or keypad fatigue → parsing / annual reminder (Upsell).
8. At 90-day win → Mastery (Upsell). Cancel Mastery → Progress or Pulse (Downsell).
9. After ~100 paying → give a month, get a month (Continuity + lead getter).

### $10M at ladder prices

Assume blended Progress ~$190/year (mix of monthly and annual). $10,000,000 / $190 ≈ **52,000** paying Canadians. At a 6% free-to-paid rate that is on the order of 850,000 free accounts — a country-scale problem if Progress is the only paid rung.

The model only reaches $10M if:

- A meaningful share climb to Mastery ($350/year), and
- Annual prepay funds CAC inside 30 days, and
- Week-2 retention exists so paid traffic is not a leak.

Do not invent a new price to make the spreadsheet work. Climb the existing ladder.

### 30-day payback sketch (Progress annual)

Gross from one annual founding or list checkout: $80 or $170. Delivery cost is modeled ~$1–$4 AI / user / month — instrument it (Phase 2). If CAC (warm + content) stays near $0 in Stage 1, payback is immediate. If later paid CAC is $80, founding annual still clears; monthly $17 does not. That is why annual is the default.

## Compliance guardrails

Copied in spirit from AGENTS.md. If a playbook idea fights this list, the list wins.

- Education and analysis, not advice. Charlie is "your AI money guide."
- Never: advisor / planner, personalized asset allocation, which account to prioritize, buy/sell a security, promised returns or dollars saved, implied human CFP/CIM review.
- Guarantee: weekly log + monthly snapshot + Score only.
- PostHog / Sentry / logs: user IDs and counts only. No amounts, names, emails, or free-text finance.
- Prices only from the AGENTS.md table.
- Mastery, Club, Academy stay hidden until `MASTERY_PUBLIC` flips.
- First reports are real-time. Never Batch API or a review queue that delays what the user sees.

## Decisions required from the owner

Record the answer in [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md) Phase 1F / Phase 4 before an agent implements it.

1. **Cancel downsell (billing).** On cancel, do we drop straight to Pulse, offer remaining time, or offer founding-price continuity if seats remain? Agents will not guess.
2. **When `MASTERY_PUBLIC` flips.** Suggested gate: Club can seat 30–50 members the same day. Confirm.
3. **Founder-led content.** Own voice vs hired VO. Never TTS. Confirm before Phase 3 production spend.
4. **Waitlist floor before live.** Plan says 1,000 emails; do not block a green Phase 0 for a round number unless the owner says so here.
5. **Kill number for content.** After 90 days of posting, fewer than `[X]` signups → change format. Write X when Phase 3 starts.
